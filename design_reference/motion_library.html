Motion library

Ten interaction patterns used on high-end product sites, built for this dashboard. Every one is live: hover, click and replay them. Each card says where it belongs.

1. Rolling digits

Use for: every headline number (value at risk, value kept)
Value at risk
0123456789 ,
Each digit is a column of 0 to 9 that slides to its value, with a small delay per digit so the number rolls from left to right.

2. Masked word reveal

Use for: the headline sentence at the top of each page

{{w.t}}

Each word rises out of a hidden line, 55ms after the one before. Key figures are tinted.
